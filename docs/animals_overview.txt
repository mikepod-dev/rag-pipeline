The animal kingdom includes millions of species, ranging from simple sponges to complex mammals. Mammals are warm-blooded vertebrates characterized by hair or fur and the production of milk for their young. Dogs and cats are among the most common domesticated mammals kept as pets worldwide. Unlike dogs, which are pack animals descended from wolves, cats retain more solitary hunting instincts from their wild ancestors. Wild cats, unlike domestic cats, are generally not kept as pets due to their unpredictable temperament. Some species of wild dogs, such as the African wild dog, remain undomesticated to this day and are considered endangered.
